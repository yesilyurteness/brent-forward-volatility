## 6. Primary hypothesis family (8 tests) — the central inferential result

Two claims × four horizons: **HAR vs HAR-X** (do the exogenous variables contribute) and **HAR-X vs XGBoost** (does the nonlinear model contribute). Holm (FWER), Benjamini-Hochberg (FDR, valid under positive dependence/PRDS) and Benjamini-Yekutieli (FDR, valid under any dependence structure; BH × c(m), c(8) = 2.718) within the family, over 8 tests. Loss: squared error. HAC: Newey-West, Bartlett, L = h-1 (declared in advance). HLN: `DM* = DM * sqrt((n+1-2h+h(h-1)/n)/n), t(n-1)`. DM sign: negative = first model better. **The Holm, BH and BY corrections are applied to the HLN p-value** (not to the raw DM p). BY is computed in this file; BH is reproduced with the same function and verified against the stored value of `08_dm_test.py` (assert). The sign test is a fold-level binomial (H0: p=0.5, two-sided); it uses no HAC/normality assumption. DM is on the pooled series (forecasts of models retrained every year), not the fold mean; hence the pooled RMSE difference differs from the fold-mean difference in Section 1 and changes sign at h=66/126 for HAR vs HAR-X.

Transparency: the family definition was formalized after the tests (it is not a pre-registration); the comparisons were chosen not according to p-values but according to the claims declared in Stages 5–6.

### 11c. Causality checks

- **Prefix-invariance:** the features were recomputed with the data cut at rows 3000 and 4000; all rows before the cut are identical at **zero tolerance** to those computed with the full data (2/2 passed).
- **Publication sensitivity:** in 40 random rows, all GPR observations not yet published at that row's date were perturbed (136–4065 observations per row). Publication-aligned arm: **40/40 unchanged**. Control arm (timestamp-aligned): **33/40 changed**.
- **Mechanism of the 7 unchanged rows in the control arm:** the timestamp arm uses at row t the observation dated t−1, and the perturbation is applied only to observations not published up to t−1. The unchanged rows are exactly the rows in which the t−1 observation had already been published by t−1 (6 Tuesday, 1 Wednesday; the t−1 of the Tuesday rows is the Monday observation published the same day, the Wednesday row is the Labor Day week). The publication calendar predicts the control arm's result correctly in **40/40** rows.

### 11d. Dependence of the two sign tests at h=22

## 12. Floor frequency, QLIKE and per-fold smearing (publication-aligned)

No retraining; everything comes from the saved prediction and fold files. QLIKE is descriptive only: no DM or sign test was run with the QLIKE loss, the primary family is fixed at 8 tests.

### 12a. How often the prediction floor binds

The level-scale OLS forecasts (HAR, HAR-X, the ablation steps) and Hybrid H3 are floored at the **minimum of the fold's training target**: `max(forecast, min(y_train))` (train-only). The log-scale HAR-log and HAR-X-log forecasts are structurally positive because they are `exp(·) × smearing`; no floor is applied to the forecast (0 by definition). (The `LOG_FLOOR = 1e-4` applied to the *regressors* of the log specifications is a separate thing and is not counted here.)

**Detection method.** The prediction files carry no floor flag. A floored row was detected as a row where the forecast is **exactly equal** to that fold's recorded floor (`bench_folds_all.pred_floor`, for H3 `hybrid_folds_all.pred_floor`). Checks (assert):
- In every fold the number of equalities is identical to the counter recorded at fit time (`n_clipped_har`, `n_clipped_har_x`, ablation `n_clipped`, H3 `n_floored`).
- No chance equality: in the models without a floor (HAR-log, HAR-X-log, GARCH, past-volatility) the number of forecasts exactly equal to the fold floor is 0. Yet HAR-log in 18, HAR-X-log in 100 rows gives forecasts **below** the floor; so the equality arises only from the `max()` operation.
- In the floored models the closest non-floored forecast is 3.83e-06 (relative 0.043%) above the floor; a continuous OLS forecast equalling the floor bit for bit by chance is practically impossible.
- The HAR-X used in the package (hybrid file) is floored on the same rows.

- **Expiry calendar:** ICE Brent rule (up to February 2016 the 15-day rule, from March 2016 the month-before rule; ICE contract specification and Circular 15/235).
